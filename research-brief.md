# research-brief.md
## Research question

Does creating a calendar/planner based on their class syllabus help with time management and help get better grades?

## Short answer

using a calendar is a short term planning that helps with time management and positively affects the grades. Keeping a calendar   helps students stay on top of their assignments and feel less overwhelmed by deadlines. Studies found that time management was associated with lower stress and greater wellbeing

## Claim 1: Time management practices

### The claim I checked

Using a calendar promotes time management and that helps students perform better in class and be less stressed
### What the source says
[https://sdlab.fas.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum5291/files/sdlab/files/hoff_macan_1990_time_management_perceived_stress_and_performance.pdf](https://sdlab.fas.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum5291/files/sdlab/files/hoff_macan_1990_time_management_perceived_stress_and_performance.pdf)

“Students who perceived control of their time reported significantly greater evaluations of their performance, greater work and life satisfaction, less role ambiguity, less role overload, and fewer job-induced and somatic tensions.”
### The corrected claim

The source matches my claim

### Why this matters for my workflow

Just adding dates on the calendar will not automatically improve performance. The student also needs to study for the assignments.
### The rule, input, or check this supports

At each weekly review, if you don’t feel in control of time, block study times for the coming days

## Claim 2: GPA

### The claim I checked

time management via a calendar/panner positively influences GPA
### What the source says

[https://psycnet.apa.org/doiLanding?doi=10.1037%2F0022-0663.83.3.405](https://psycnet.apa.org/doiLanding?doi=10.1037%2F0022-0663.83.3.405)

“Regression analyses showed that 2 time-management components were significant predictors of cumulative GPA (_R_–2 = .21) and accounted for more variance than did SAT scores (increment in _R_–2 = .05). It is concluded that time-management practices may influence college achievement.”

### The corrected claim

Just planning the assignments will not help, students need to block time to study to get better grades.

### Why this matters for my workflow

Studying for an assignment is key and only planning doesn’t increase GPA.

### The rule, input, or check this supports

For every item due within the next week, there must be at least one specific study block scheduled in the calendar before that due date. Planning only the deadlines doesn't count (Britton & Tesser, 1991)
